# Consequential Action Approval Policy

## Usually reversible

- Open a page or tab
- Scroll and read
- Expand a menu
- Fill a non-sensitive draft field that has not been submitted

## Requires approval enforcement

- Submit a form
- Send a message or email
- Publish content
- Purchase or book
- Delete or archive external data
- Upload or download a file
- Change account, privacy, billing, or security settings

## Never accept through chat

- Passwords
- One-time authentication codes
- Recovery codes
- Payment card details
- Session cookies or access tokens
- Private keys

Approval is valid only for the exact reviewed action and page state. Material changes invalidate the approval.
